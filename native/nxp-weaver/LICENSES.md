Platform files retain their original copyright and license headers.
Bionic headers and CRT sources include their BSD license notices;
Binder, libbase, liblog and Stable AIDL sources retain their individual
notices. Additional notices are under licenses/.

The platform libc++ headers and public Clang compiler distribution are
covered by licenses/clang-r596125-NOTICE, which includes the LLVM Apache

from the official stock image. They are not included in this source kit.

pinned public repositories and are not included in the source kit.
