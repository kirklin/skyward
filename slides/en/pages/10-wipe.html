Wiping data

Every choice says
what it wipes.

Wipe only app caches, the ART cache, logs or downloaded updates, or reset the phone. A factory reset leaves the system and its installed updates alone.

data partition

  • Internal storage/data/media: the photos, videos and files in /sdcard
  • App cachescache and code_cache in each app's data, and cache in Android/data. Apps make them again as they need them
  • System caches/data/system/package_cache, /data/resource-cache and /data/cache, made again at the next start
  • Downloaded updatesSystem update packages in /data/ota_package

Wiping app data or app caches needs the data partition decrypted first. What is wiped can't be brought back.
