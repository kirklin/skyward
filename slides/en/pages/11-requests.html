Replacing the stock recovery

Skyward does what
the system asks.

The system writes its request to the misc partition and restarts into recovery. Skyward reads it, carries it out, then reboots or powers off as asked.

The system asks

Skyward

  • Factory reset in Settings--wipe_data
    Wipes all data, then reboots
    or powers off on its own
  • The system can't start--prompt_and_wipe_data
    Offers to try again
    or to factory reset
  • adb reboot sideload--sideload
    Waits for the computer
    to send a package
  • adb reboot fastboot--fastboot
    Starts fastbootd
